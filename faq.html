Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about my services, process, and expertise

What technologies do you specialize in?

I specialize in MERN Stack (MongoDB, Express.js, React.js, Node.js), Laravel (PHP), WordPress, and Shopify. I also work with Next.js, MySQL, and various DevOps tools like Git and Docker.

What's your typical project turnaround time?

Most projects start within 1–2 weeks of agreement. A typical MVP (Minimum Viable Product) is delivered in 4–6 weeks, while complex enterprise solutions may take 8–12 weeks. I always provide a clear timeline during our initial consultation.

Do you work with startups and small businesses?

Absolutely! I love working with startups and small businesses. I can help you build an MVP, scale your product, or create a professional web presence. I offer flexible pricing packages for early-stage companies.

How do you handle revisions and feedback?

I offer 2 rounds of revisions included in every project. We use tools like Trello or Notion for feedback tracking, and I provide weekly progress updates. Additional revision rounds can be arranged if needed.

What about post-launch support and maintenance?

Every project comes with 30 days of free post-launch support. After that, I offer affordable monthly maintenance packages starting at $200/month, which include updates, security patches, backups, and minor changes.

How do you ensure SEO-friendly development?

I implement SEO best practices from the ground up: semantic HTML5, SSR with Next.js, optimized images, proper meta tags, schema markup, fast loading speeds (Core Web Vitals), and mobile-first responsive design.

What is your payment structure?

I typically work with a 50% upfront payment and 50% upon completion. For larger projects (6+ weeks), I use milestone-based payments (25% per milestone). I accept bank transfers, PayPal, and Wise.

Can you work with existing codebases or teams?

Yes! I have extensive experience joining ongoing projects, understanding existing codebases, and collaborating with remote teams. I use Git, Slack, and Jira for seamless team integration.
