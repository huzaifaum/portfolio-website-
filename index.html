Got Questions?

Everything you need to know.

Here are answers to the most common questions about my process, timeline, pricing and how we can work together.

What is your development process?

I start with a discovery phase to understand your goals, then design a solution, build it with clean code, test thoroughly, and deliver a polished product. I keep you updated at every stage.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. A simple business website can take 2–4 weeks, while a complex e-commerce or custom application may take 6–12 weeks. I provide a timeline after understanding your requirements.

Do you offer maintenance and support?

Yes. I offer ongoing maintenance, updates, and support packages to ensure your website stays secure, fast and up-to-date after launch.

What technologies do you work with?

I work with HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, React.js, Node.js, PHP & Laravel, WordPress, and more. I choose the right technology based on your project needs.

How much does a project cost?

Costs vary based on project complexity. I provide transparent quotes after understanding your requirements. I offer flexible options to fit different budgets.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Absolutely. I can refresh your existing website with a modern design, improved performance, better user experience, and updated technology stack.

Still have questions? I'm happy to help. Reach out anytime.
